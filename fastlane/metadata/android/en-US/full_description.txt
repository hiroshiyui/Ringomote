Hello, it's me, Ringomote.

I turn your phone into a remote for Apple TV, compatible with the Siri Remote (1st generation). I talk to the Apple TV directly over Bluetooth Low Energy, presenting myself as a Bluetooth input device, just as the physical remote does. No Wi-Fi network, Apple account or bridge device is involved, I connect to no server, and I collect no data.

What I offer:

* A touch surface: swipe to move the highlight, tap to select.
* Menu, TV (tap for Home, hold for Control Center), Play/Pause, Volume up/down and Mute buttons.
* Pairing from the Apple TV's own Bluetooth settings, with the steps shown on screen.
* Optionally, waking a sleeping Apple TV over your Wi-Fi network: find it with Bonjour, pair once with the code the Apple TV shows, then tap Wake.
* TalkBack support, keyboard and D-pad control, and layouts that survive large fonts.
* English, 台灣漢語 and 日本語.

What I can't do: Siri voice input.

Requirements: a phone running Android 9 or later with Bluetooth Low Energy, and an Apple TV that works with the Siri Remote (1st generation) or Bluetooth input devices.
