Hello, it's me, Ringomote.

I turn your phone into a remote for Apple TV, compatible with the Siri Remote (1st generation). I talk to the Apple TV over your Wi-Fi network with the same protocol the iPhone and iPad Remote use: find it with Bonjour, pair once with the code it shows, and I control it from then on. No Apple account, bridge device or server is involved, I connect to no server, and I collect no data.

What I offer:

* A touch surface: swipe to move the highlight, tap to select.
* Menu, TV (tap for Home, hold for Control Center), Play/Pause and Volume up/down buttons.
* Waking the Apple TV when it sleeps.
* TalkBack support, keyboard and D-pad control, and layouts that survive large fonts.
* English, 台灣漢語 and 日本語.

What I can't do: Siri voice input, and Mute, which the protocol has no command for.

Requirements: a phone running Android 9 or later on the same Wi-Fi network as the Apple TV, and an Apple TV that works with the iPhone or iPad Remote.
